## Privacy, recovery and distribution scope

Encryption includes FTS and derived database content; macOS Keychain and private recovery-key workflows are exercised. Provider/project/directory/topic/conversation exclusions apply to retained sources and future imports, preserve overlapping/manual controls, and invalidate generated content. Source/observation deletion, edited-projection conflicts, backup key rotation and retained review history have regression coverage. [Privacy matching semantics](privacy.md) · [Security boundaries](../SECURITY.md).

Older 4.14/OpenSSL encrypted backups restored on 4.19/CommonCrypto without changing the original. This is observed process-interruption coverage, not exhaustive power-loss testing. [Recovery](recovery.md).

The historical 2,000-conversation / 32,000-message performance check passed on this Mac; its measured schema/version is stated in [performance.md](performance.md). Cargo-audit 0.22.2 found zero advisories/warnings in 189 locked dependencies against RustSec commit `999ec89d5d3f10f20be14750ec8c2d8b44e0ac9b` (2026-10-17). The notice inventory covers 153 Rust dependency packages, native notices, Rust library notices and the unmodified MPL source archive. Neither is an independent security/legal certification.

The advertised initial platform is this Apple Silicon macOS target. Other operating systems and narrow-viewport layouts remain unverified. CI is configured but unobserved remotely. Publishing, Developer ID signing/notarization and a repository-specific private reporting channel are separate distribution setup after the user creates a remote; this local task does not authorize publication. ## Review identity and deletion

A synthetic audit reproduced a rejected finding reappearing under different canonical wording against the same statement. The initial transactional overlap guard prevented that bypass but could leave distinct facts pending. The current implementation compares complete identities, rechecks a proposed existing claim against new source quotations, permits explicitly distinct facts sharing a quote and rechecks a knowledge/review snapshot at commit. Ambiguous reviewed overlaps stay pending. Offline regressions cover durable rejection, distinct facts, concurrency, recurrence without confirmation and failed-reuse fallback. Its ten v16 model identity development cases pass after correcting a failed scope case; v20 and real-data acceptance remain under evaluation. [Identity rules](architecture.md#canonical-identity-reuse).

A separate audit reproduced deleted observations returning under fresh canonical IDs. Schema 5 now blocks re-extraction from unchanged cited spans using opaque message-digest/range tombstones, without retaining deleted claim text. The encrypted restore/revision regression covers shorter overlapping UTF-8 quotes, changed kinds, preserved non-overlapping facts and genuinely changed source text. Whole-source deletion removes no-longer-needed range metadata. This does not establish a permanent semantic ban across different or changed source messages. [Deletion scope](privacy.md).

No real user feedback was reset or manufactured.


The broader v16 attempt completed 38/40 sources in 1,380.68 seconds; both pending sources completed on a checkpointed retry with a 240-second Qwen generation cap (still 4,096 output tokens). Metadata/verification keep shorter deadlines, and errors identify generation/support/context/matching stages. The resulting review copy covers all 40 conversations / 3,061 messages / 47 revisions, with 36 visible claims and zero confirmations. Two apparent recurrences were exact cumulative Kiro history copies; conservative origin counting correctly reduces useful real recurrence to zero.

Full-corpus agent review still fails usefulness: only 1/5 of the current top five is both accurate and useful. Mixed/clipped evidence promoted questions and uncited attributes into preferences; a discovery-authorization reply was misread as a decision. A private diagnostic of five disputed findings plus a synthetic control found that fast verification accepted every item even after clarification and output-field reordering. Reasoning rejected four disputed unsupported claims while retaining the control; it still accepted the routine response-format task as a decision. These are diagnostics, not independent accuracy estimates. The verification policy needs further work before final packaging.
