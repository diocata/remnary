Two inferences had only one independently relevant supporting message; two topics came from a steering-initialization task template. This fails the grounding target. One overlapping decision/constraint pair is also noted in the private assessment. There were **zero genuine cross-source recurring groups and zero owner confirmations**; copied Kiro histories count once.

The owner delegated evaluation without further questions. Ratings are agent assessments, not independent user research or owner endorsement. Acceptance targets remain at least 90% factual grounding and three of the top five useful, with exact provenance, safe reimports and feedback/deletion behavior. No real feedback is fabricated to improve the score. V35 needs fresh full-corpus extraction and review; targeted removal of four bad findings is not a complete quality result.
